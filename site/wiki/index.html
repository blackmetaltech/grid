How a job works

  1. Read the work order. The place, three people on the radio, the goal in plain words, the pass conditions with their numbers and the three lamps you can earn.
  2. Build. The tray holds the level's parts, each with its price; the budget tag keeps the running total.
  3. Run the day. The lever runs the 24-hour clock. Voltages, loadings and frequency move on the dials and the map.
  4. Read the shift report. It explains what happened in your own numbers and what to try next.
