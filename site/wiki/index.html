What is in the game now

Version
1.18.0, "Bay and Mainland". Alpha. This wiki is built from that release.
Campaign
A prologue and 36 jobs in 8 chapters and a finale. All of them can be played, from Ohm's law on a beach to a province.
Career
Ten years as a district engineer, one quarter at a time. Nine districts, and a weekly district that is the same for everyone.
In the new 3D look
The title, chapter 1 (WO-01 to WO-03), chapter 2 (WO-05, WO-29, WO-30), chapter 3 (WO-07 to WO-09), the finale (WO-28).
Still in the classic 2D look
The prologue, chapter 2 (WO-04, WO-06), chapter 4 (WO-10 to WO-13), chapter 5 (WO-14 to WO-18), chapter 6 (WO-19 to WO-22), chapter 7 (WO-23 to WO-27), chapter 8 (WO-31 to WO-36). The job board marks them "Classic".
Platforms
Linux and Windows and macOS. The macOS build is untested: no one has opened it on a Mac.

The campaign is complete. What is not built yet

Every chapter, job and the Career exist. What is left is mostly the look:

  • The new 3D look for the prologue, the benches of chapter 2 and chapters 4 to 8. Those jobs still draw in the classic 2D look. The design for this is in docs/LOOK2.md; no date is set.
  • A Career screen laid over the district, with the board as a drawer. Today the Career has the glass panels of the new look on a flat map.
  • Woods and a landmark for the Mainland towns, and a busier first day in the finale. These are open items from the 1.18 review.

The design docs also list ideas that are not scheduled: black-start jobs, a level editor, other languages.

What nobody has checked yet

  • Nobody has listened to the sound. Every cue is made in code and measured, not heard.
  • The Windows build is made and started on Linux for every release, and has not been played by a person.
  • The two Mainland map jobs in 3D (WO-05 and WO-29) have been checked by tests and screenshots, not played by hand at full speed.
