Goal

Night shift. Line T5 is back from maintenance: close its breaker. It is the only thing you can do.

If it fails

This job is a scene: it has no pass or fail and awards no lamps.

On the job board this job is listed under "Chapter 1 · The Island".

Coach steps (hints that show how to build)
  1. Line T5 is back from maintenance. Close the glowing breaker.Closing a line ties two parts of the grid together; power now flows where it did not before.
  2. Watch the lines.A line carrying more than its rating heats up and trips on its timer; its flow lands on the next line.
