Keep the Lights On

WO-28 Keep the Lights On

State control room, 26 buses, three days

Three days on the whole state: a heatwave, a storm, a holiday. The reinforcement budget cannot buy every fix: choose the parts that keep the province lit and Saltmarsh Island bright through the night of Day 2.

On the radio

  • ADA Day one is the record heat. Every air conditioner in the province is on, and the long lines sag the voltage at the far end.
  • TOBI Day two is the storm. A 400 kV line, the biggest unit and a transformer will trip, and lightning will take the one feeder to Saltmarsh. Nia's exam is the morning after.
  • VANCE Day three is the holiday: full sun, no factories. Too much voltage and too little inertia. Spend what you must, no more.

Steps

  1. Day 1 is a heatwave: the 400 kV bus at Eastgate and the 132 kV tail to Harbour and the island sag towards 0.95 pu, and Day 3's empty holiday lifts them. The card at the top right shelves the voltage parts with their prices. Which of them holds the far end up?Reactive power cannot travel far: the voltage drop along a line is mostly X times the reactive flow, so the capacitor has to stand near the load.
  2. Day 2 trips the biggest unit, 660 MW, and Day 3's sunset falls on a thin fleet. Only what is already spinning or stored answers in the first minutes; the rest is energy not served, and the three days allow at most 131 MWh of it. Buy headroom from the reserve shelf, or judge that you can go without and spend on the island.When a unit trips, the units still running must pick up its output at once. Headroom you do not hold cannot be summoned.
  3. Lightning opens the one causeway line to Saltmarsh Island at 23:30 on Day 2, and the crews close it at 06:00. In those six and a half hours the island draws about 92 MWh. The island shelf has lines and batteries at very different prices: pick a way to keep the school lit.N-1: after any single trip every load must still have a path. A battery is a path in time: its energy must cover every hour until the repair.
  4. Run the three days. Watch the day indicator and the event ticker.The stars are earned over all 288 blocks, not at any one moment.
